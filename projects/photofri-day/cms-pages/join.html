Get In

One follow. That's the whole sign-up.

Follow @participate@photofri.day from wherever you already post — Mastodon, Pixelfed, Vernissage, SnapSmack, anything that speaks ActivityPub. Paste that handle into your app's search box and press follow.

From then on the weekly prompt arrives in your feed, the same as any other account you follow. Nothing to install, no account to make here, no password to forget. Your photos stay on your own server; we just point people at them.

  1. Follow @participate@photofri.day.
  2. Watch for the prompt — it lands Friday morning, a week before it's due.
  3. Post your entry with that week's hashtag, from your own account, any time that week. Five entries maximum.
  4. We boost it so everyone else following sees it in their own feed. Likes on that boost are what count.

Not sure what any of that means? How It Works spells it out, and the FAQ covers the rest. To leave, unfollow — that's the whole exit too.

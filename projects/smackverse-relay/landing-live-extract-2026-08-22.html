SMACKVERSE Relay

The fan-out relay for the SnapSmack photo-blogging network.

This is an ActivityPub relay — an Application actor, not a person. SnapSmack blogs opt in, and each member's public posts fan out to the others, so the whole network's timeline fills in without everyone following everyone by hand.

What it does not do

It stores no images and no private data. The relay passes along activity IDs and text only — photos always load from the origin blog. Nothing here was ever private: it relays public posts, and membership is entirely opt-in.

What we stand for

  • Open software — SnapSmack is ours to run and yours to fork.
  • Open protocols — plain ActivityPub, so we federate with the whole fediverse, not a walled garden.
  • Open communication — reach that isn't throttled by an algorithm or an ad auction.
  • Self-determination — your blog is your identity, your data, and your call. You own it; we just help it travel.

Endpoints

/actor · /nodeinfo · /inbox (signed POST)

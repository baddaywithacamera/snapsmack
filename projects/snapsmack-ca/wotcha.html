SnapSmack Picks Up Where PixelPost Left Off — April 2026

If you used PixelPost, you already know what SnapSmack is. One photo, one post, your words around it, your site, your rules. No feed burying you. No algorithm deciding who sees your work. No terms of service changing on a Tuesday because someone in marketing had a thought.

PixelPost did that beautifully until development stopped in 2009. Sixteen years ago. The community kept it alive on fumes for a decade before spam and abandonment finally won.

SnapSmack starts where PixelPost stopped — and keeps going. Built from scratch on current PHP and MySQL by Sean McCormick and Claude (Anthropic) — one set of hands, no prior experience shipping software, zero apologies. Solo mode works exactly like PixelPost: one image, one post, chronological, yours. Carousel mode handles multi-image posts when one frame isn't enough. A skin designer lets you build and preview your own look against live content without touching code. An AI assistant helps when you're staring at a blank caption field at midnight.

The companion tools are built for the hobbyist who punches like Mike Tyson. A batch processor handles the jobs that would make a shared host spontaneously combust. A backup tool moves your content between cloud services directly — no middleman, no memory limits, no drama. And the forum requires a handshake before you can post, because we watched what open comments did last time.

Sixteen years is long enough to wait.

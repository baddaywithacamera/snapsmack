# Security Policy

SnapSmack is built for photographers who trust it with their archives — some of
them irreplaceable. We treat security reports as a favour, not a nuisance.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Email: security@snapsmack.ca**

Please include:
- What you found, where (URL, file, or tool), and what it lets someone do.
- Steps to reproduce — a plain list is perfect.
- Whether you believe it is being exploited in the wild.

Please do NOT open a public issue, post about it, or demonstrate it on someone
else's install before we've had a chance to fix it.

## What to expect

- **Acknowledgement within 72 hours.** A real reply from a person, not a bot.
- We investigate, fix, and ship security fixes ahead of all other work.
- You'll be told when the fix is released, and credited if you want credit.
- No legal threats for good-faith research against your own install or our
  test instances. Testing against other people's live sites without their
  permission is not good-faith research.

## Scope

- The SnapSmack CMS (this repository) and its update/packaging pipeline.
- The desktop companion tools distributed with it.
- The federation (ActivityPub) surface of SnapSmack installs.

Out of scope: third-party servers SnapSmack federates with, and social
engineering of individual site owners.

## Supported versions

Alpha-track software: only the **current release** receives security fixes.
Fleet installs update centrally; self-hosted installs should stay current.
